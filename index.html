The challenge

South pole missions face extreme lighting and communication limits. Small changes in site or date can swing power and Earth visibility. This tool turns that into clear, comparable visuals for planners, teachers and the public.

Values are computed in your browser with an approximate model of the Sun and Earth positions, without terrain. They are for exploration. Use NASA tools such as JPL Horizons and Moon Trek for mission-grade numbers.
